# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are acting in good faith. Argue about the code, not about the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on the work. "This drops the colour profile, which changes how the image renders" is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Accept that other people have context you do not. A decision that looks wrong often has a reason behind it; ask before assuming.
- Take a correction gracefully, and give one gracefully.
- Respect that contributors are volunteering their time, including when they are slow to respond or decline your idea.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private information. Sexual attention or imagery is not appropriate anywhere in this project.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages — and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses escalate as needed: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. The maintainer decides, and will explain the reasoning to the person concerned.
